Sealed Bench

Private AI evals, publicly audited on NEAR. Owners commit a sealed test set and every model run; NEAR randomness picks which items must be revealed; the contract checks each reveal against NEAR AI Cloud TEE signatures and re-grades it.
How to read a claim. "Owner-reported score X; 20/20 sampled entries verified" means each sampled entry matched the committed set, the committed run, a signature from a pinned NEAR AI TEE signer, and the committed grade. If the owner had misreported at least 10% of entries, a 20-item sample would catch that with probability at least 87.8% (1−0.920), or about 90.5% exactly when 20 distinct items are drawn from 100. This holds per run. It is not the probability that the score is correct, and it says nothing about benchmark quality.
